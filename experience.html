Robotics and Controls Intern · LIDSS Group

Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

Built a hardware-in-the-loop interface between an autonomous raft’s physical CORE control board and ShipSim3, enabling closed-loop controller testing without deploying a raft. Designed a multithreaded Python architecture for UDP and serial communication.

UDP forwarding took less than 1 ms; 95th-percentile serial latency was 21 ms against a 50 ms requirement. Identified a three-degree-of-freedom damping model using Savitzky–Golay filtering and linear least-squares regression, with yaw-rate NRMSE of 15% against wet-test data and 22% against independent mission data.
