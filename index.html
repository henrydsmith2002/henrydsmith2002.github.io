ABOUT

Working across the hardware–software boundary.

I’m interested in engineering problems where software senses, simulates, or controls a real physical system.

At Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, I connected an autonomous raft controller to a simulation and modeled its motion from experimental data. At BYU’s Robotics and Dynamics Lab, I worked on control software and integration for a dual-robot system.

I now research hybrid UAV control in BYU’s MAGICC Lab. My technical interests span robotics, control systems, autonomous aerospace systems, embedded software, and simulation.
